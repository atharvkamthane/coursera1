# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple interest calculator determines the interest earned on a principal amount over a given period at a fixed annual interest rate.

## Formula

**Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100**

Where:
- **Principal** is the initial amount of money.
- **Rate** is the annual interest rate in percent.
- **Time** is the duration in years.

The total amount after applying simple interest is:

**Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest**

## Example

For a principal of ₹10,000, an annual interest rate of 5%, and a time period of 2 years:

- Simple Interest = (10,000 × 5 × 2) / 100
- Simple Interest = ₹1,000
- Total Amount = ₹11,000

## Purpose

This calculator provides a straightforward way to understand and calculate simple interest for basic financial calculations.
